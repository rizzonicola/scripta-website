Open-source, local-first note-taking. Raw Markdown, instant search, self-hosted sync. On all your devices.

Scripta is an open-source Markdown notes app for people who want to write without strings attached: no mandatory account, no forced cloud. It's local-first: your notes live on your device, with instant SQLite-backed search and optional sync to a server you choose. Free for Android, iOS, macOS, Windows and Linux.

Built for writing

Markdown editor

Syntax highlighting, smart toolbar and an instant reading view.

Local-first

Instant startup and lightning search via SQLite WAL. Works offline.

Sync

Async sync with self-hosted backends, Last-Write-Wins resolution.

Nested folders

Favorites, pinned notes and drag & drop.

Your data

ZIP or Markdown import/export. No lock-in.

Cross-platform

Android, iOS, macOS, Windows and Linux.
